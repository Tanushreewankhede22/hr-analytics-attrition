# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data to understand workforce patterns and employee attrition.

The analysis was performed using **MySQL, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI**.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition
- Identify departments with different attrition rates
- Analyze attrition by job role
- Understand the relationship between overtime and attrition
- Analyze attrition across age groups
- Compare average monthly income across departments
- Analyze job satisfaction, work-life balance, and tenure
- Perform data-quality checks
- Build an interactive HR dashboard

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **MySQL** — Data analysis and SQL queries
- **Microsoft Excel** — Data analysis and dashboard
- **Power BI** — Interactive dashboard and visualization
- **GitHub** — Project documentation and portfolio

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### Department Analysis
Analyzed employee count, employees who left, and attrition rate across departments.

### Job Role Analysis
Compared employee attrition across different job roles.

### Overtime Analysis
Compared attrition rates between employees working overtime and those who do not.

### Age Group Analysis
Analyzed attrition across different age groups.

### Income Analysis
Compared average monthly income across departments.

### Employee Satisfaction
Analyzed job satisfaction and environment satisfaction in relation to attrition.

### Work-Life Balance
Analyzed employee attrition across different work-life balance levels.

### Tenure Analysis
Analyzed attrition based on employees' years at the company.

### Data Quality
Performed checks for missing values, duplicate employee IDs, and attrition categories.

## 📊 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard contains:

- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Attrition Rate
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Years at Company
- Attrition Rate by Department
- Attrition Rate by Overtime
- Attrition Rate by Age Group
- Average Monthly Income by Department
